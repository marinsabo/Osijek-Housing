# NNN. Short noun phrase naming the decision

<!--
How to use:
1. Copy: cp docs/adr/template.md docs/adr/NNN-short-title.md
   NNN = next number, zero-padded (001, 002, ...). Numbers are never reused.
2. Fill each section, then delete these comments.
3. Write in full sentences, as if talking to a developer who joins next year.
   Bullets only for layout, never as sentence fragments.
4. Keep it to one or two pages. One ADR = one decision.
5. Open a PR; Status becomes Accepted when the team approves it.
-->

## Context

<!--
The forces at play: technical, team, time, money, project-specific. State
facts in neutral language, not arguments for your choice.
- What problem or question forces a decision now?
- What constraints apply (people, hours/week, experience, server, budget, deadlines)?
- Which forces pull against each other? Name the tension.
- Which options were realistic? Describe each fairly in a sentence or two.
-->

## Decision

<!--
Full sentences, active voice: "We will ..."
- What exactly will we do?
- What rules follow from it (who may do what, what is not allowed)?
- Why this option over the others, given the forces above?
-->

## Consequences

<!--
ALL consequences, not just good ones.
- What becomes easier?
- What becomes harder, riskier or costs more? How do we live with that?
- What does everyone now have to do (or stop doing)?
- What would make us revisit this decision?
Consequences often become the Context of a later ADR.
-->
